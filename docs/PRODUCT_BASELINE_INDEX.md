# Product baseline — Nablon + Becoming
Status: review required; this index does not replace approved primary sources.

## Approved primary source
- Becoming Architecture v1.0, approved by owner 2026-11-02:
  https://github.com/yuranext-tech/becoming/blob/main/docs/architecture/Becoming_Architecture_v1.0_APPROVED_2026-11-02.md
- In that architecture, Becoming is the independent behavioral engine; Nablon is a training interface, not the analytical engine.
- The architecture requires realistic scenarios, independent free-form actions, observable consequences, voluntary alternatives after completion, and delayed transfer checks.

## Nablon editorial checks (not yet individually ratified as a complete frozen baseline)
1. A human-readable, interesting, relatable story, with full original wording preserved for review.
2. Difficulty in choosing a course of action, not deciphering terminology.
3. Independent action, not just choosing the expected answer button.
4. Gradual complexity and optional help without spoiling the measured decision.
5. Actions, outcomes and interpretations are separately recorded.
6. A single outcome does not prove learning or a stable trait.
7. No change to an approved experiment's frozen rules without owner authorization.
8. Becoming observations, hypotheses, predictions and intervention benefits are separate claims.
